The teachings do not say the moon changes your mood or health. They give timings, such as when a month starts, which days to fast and which days suit cupping, and they describe the moon as a sign, not a cause. Studies on the moon's effect on sleep and mood are mixed, and any effect found is small. If you are unwell, take medication or have a medical condition, check with a doctor before fasting.
